🧭 Prioritization & Delegation Decision Matrix

The exact decision frameworks tested heavily on the PNLE:

  • 1. ABC Principle: Airway (stridor, swelling) > Breathing (dyspnea, O2 sat) > Circulation (hypotension, bleeding).
  • 2. Acute vs. Chronic: New onset confusion or sudden pain takes priority over long-standing COPD or osteoarthritis.
  • 3. Unstable vs. Stable: Vital sign shifts or hemorrhaging clients precede scheduled medication administration.
  • 4. Scope & Delegation: Never delegate what you EAT: Evaluate, Assess, Teach!

Republic Act No. 9173 (Philippine Nursing Act of 2002) - Core Provisions

Statutory mandates governing licensure, board composition, and standards of nursing practice in the Philippines.

Board of Nursing (BON)

1 Chairperson and 6 Members (Total: 7). Appointed by the President of the Philippines. 3-year term, eligible for one reappointment (max 6 years).

Board Member Qualifications

RN with Master's degree in nursing, natural born Filipino, at least 10 years continuous practice with last 5 years in the Philippines (Sec. 4).

Passing Rating Standard

General Average ≥ 75% with NO rating below 60% in any subject. Conditional status if ≥75% overall but <60% in a subject (retake within 2 years).

Scope of Practice (Sec. 28)

Special training explicitly required for: 1) Intravenous injections, and 2) Suturing of perineal lacerations.
